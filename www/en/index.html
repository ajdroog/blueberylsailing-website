2,100 NAUTICAL MILES WITHOUT A MAST.

In the middle of the Pacific our mast breaks. What follows is a journey of weeks on a self-built jury rig, with one goal: to arrive safely. We only managed it as a team. Now we tell that story on stage. Raw, honest and full of lessons about resilience and deciding together under pressure.

THE STORY

22 APRIL 2022,
06:00 HOURS.

Halfway across the Pacific, on our way to French Polynesia, the mast of the Blue Beryl, our Koopmans 44, snaps. Waves of two and a half metres, half the mast overboard, the mainsail in the water. There is no way back, only forward.

  1. 01

    THE BLOW

    There is no getting the mast back on board. We cut the rigging loose and watch it sink. In a few minutes a world voyage turns into a fight for survival.

  2. 02

    THE JURY RIG

    From a spinnaker pole and a storm jib we build a jury rig. Three, four knots. Beating back to the Galápagos against the wind is not an option, so we choose the long way to the Marquesas.

  3. 03

    HANGING IN THERE TOGETHER

    Day after day, watch after watch. Passing ships bring diesel, bread and courage. At home the family keeps in touch by satellite. We make every decision together. When it storms, you are only as strong as your team.

  4. 04

    LAND IN SIGHT

    After 2,100 nautical miles we sail into Hiva Oa. Safe. Exhausted. And with a story we never wanted to have, but would not want to be without now.

THE TALK

WE LOVE TO TELL IT
AT YOUR EVENT.

For sailing clubs, sports clubs, schools and networking evenings, but just as much for companies and teams. That is what we do it for. With footage from on board, honest stories and a good dose of humour, we take you out onto the ocean.

RESILIENCE

How to stay upright when the plan literally goes overboard, and what you need to keep going.

DECIDING UNDER PRESSURE

Choosing a course with incomplete information. Galápagos or the Marquesas? What do you weigh when there is no room for mistakes?

WORKING TOGETHER AS A TEAM

Two people, thirteen metres, weeks at sea. About trust, dividing roles and servant leadership: how to stay strong as a team, even when it storms.

IMPROVISING

A spinnaker pole and a storm jib became our salvation. Solving problems creatively with what you do have.

TEAMWORK

WHEN IT STORMS, YOUR TEAM IS EVERYTHING.

At sea you cannot walk away and you cannot solve everything alone. Who steers, who rests and who keeps a cool head: you agree that before it is needed. In our talk we translate that to your team. How do you stay strong together, precisely when things go against you?

ABOUT US

SANDER & YVETTE

Raised among the Frisian lakes and the Wadden Sea, we set off in 2020 on the Blue Beryl for a voyage around the world. Five years later, in September 2025, we came home to Groningen: with thousands of miles in our wake and one morning that changed everything. On stage we tell it together, each from our own perspective.

YVETTE

Yvette tells the story from the inside: what it is like when the mast goes overboard and you then spend weeks on a rolling boat, far outside your comfort zone. Honest about fear, about perseverance and about what you have in each other. She also wrote it down herself, for the magazine Zeilen.

SANDER

Sander is a project manager, entrepreneur and coach with a background in sport and leadership. He talks about the choices on board: letting the mast go, building a jury rig from a spinnaker pole and setting a course for the Marquesas.

THE SHIP

BLUE BERYL

KOOPMANS 44

A steel full-keel yacht from 1996, designed by Dick Koopmans. For five years our home, our workshop and our transport. Her name comes from the blue beryl, the aquamarine: ‘water of the sea’, a traditional talisman for sailors.
